Title: Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 on deforestation-free products (EUDR)

Key Excerpts:
"Scope: 7 commodities and derived products: cattle, cocoa, coffee, oil palm, rubber, soya, wood.
Application timeline (post-amendment):
- Large and medium-sized operators: 30 December 2025 / 30 December 2026.
- Micro and small enterprises: 30 June 2026 / 30 June 2027.
Obligated parties: Importers and traders placing relevant commodities on the EU market.
Work created: Geolocation mapping of plots of land, Due Diligence Statements (DDS) submitted to the EU Traces system, timber legality and wood species anatomical identification audits.
Physical work: Physical container sampling and microscopic/macroscopic wood species testing (performed by NVWA/Customs or ISO-accredited testing laboratories)."

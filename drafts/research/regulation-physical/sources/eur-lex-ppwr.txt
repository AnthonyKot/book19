Title: Regulation (EU) 2025/40 on packaging and packaging waste (PPWR)

Key Excerpts:
"Entry into force: 11 February 2025.
General application: 12 August 2026 (18 months after entry into force).
Key dated obligations 2026-2028:
- 12 August 2026: General application of packaging reduction, substances restrictions (PFAS in food contact packaging), and recyclability design requirements.
- 12 February 2027 (Art. 28): HORECA sector takeaway food/beverage providers must allow consumers to bring their own containers without charge.
- 1 January 2028: Phased introduction of harmonised EU sorting labels and return pictograms on packaging.
- 1 January 2030: Mandatory binding reuse targets (e.g., 40% of transport packaging, e-commerce packaging, pallet wrapping; 10% reusable beverage packaging) and 100% recyclable packaging mandate.
Obligated parties: Packaging manufacturers, brand owners, importers, distributors, HORECA businesses, e-commerce fulfillment centers."

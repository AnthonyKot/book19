# Research — regulation that creates physical or licensed work, 2026–2028 (folder drafts/research/regulation-physical)
Question: which EU and Dutch rules with dated obligations between now and 2028 create physical, licensed or on-site work in the Netherlands that a small new operator could sell, outside IT? Candidates: packaging and packaging-waste regulation (PPWR) obligations, EU deforestation regulation for importers, CBAM declarations, building energy labels and the EPBD, the Dutch building-quality act (Wkb), heat-pump and insulation subsidies (ISDE) and their installer requirements, the Omgevingswet permit regime, waste and EPR schemes, fire-safety and inspection duties, e-mobility charging obligations, water and drought rules, the labour-migrant housing rules. For each: the legal text and Dutch implementation with dates, the obligated parties, the work created, the licence or certification the operator needs (and whether its exam is Dutch-only), the incumbents, and evidence of shortage (UWV kansrijke beroepen, trade-body statements). Sources: EUR-Lex, Rijksoverheid, RVO, UWV, trade bodies.

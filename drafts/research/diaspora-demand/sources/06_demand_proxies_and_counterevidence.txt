SOURCE S33 — deaths among Ukrainian refugees (dated scenario)
Publisher: Verbond van Uitvaartorganisaties (funeral-sector trade body)
URL: https://verbondvanuitvaartzorg.nl/informatie/handreiking-en-factsheet-rond-overlijden-oekrainers-in-nederland/
Fetch date: 2026-09-03

Excerpt:
Using a July 2022 base of 70,660 Ukrainian refugees, the trade body’s factsheet projected
629–1,011 deaths per year and provided guidance for handling deaths and repatriation.

Limit: this is a dated national projection, not observed deaths, not the current protected
population and not a Randstad/belt estimate. It is used only as evidence that the sector
recognised a recurring service need; it must not be scaled to today’s population.

Excerpt:
The municipality warns that childcare facilities often have waiting lists and advises

Limit: this is general Amsterdam childcare demand, not language-specific demand. The
language rule in S30 means the apparent language-service gap is not a lawful standalone
full-day childcare opportunity.


SOURCE S35 — validity of foreign driving licences
Publisher: Netherlands Vehicle Authority (RDW)
URL: https://www.rdw.nl/en/driving-licence/foreign-driving-licence/driving-with-a-foreign-driving-licence
Fetch date: 2026-09-03

Excerpt:
EU/EFTA driving licences can normally be used in the Netherlands while valid, subject to
the RDW rules. The page also sets out temporary arrangements for people from Ukraine.

Limit: individual circumstances and validity periods vary. This is counterevidence to a
broad claim that Polish/Ukrainian newcomers necessarily need Dutch driving lessons.

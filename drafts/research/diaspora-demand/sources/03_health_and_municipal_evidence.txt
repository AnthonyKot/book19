SOURCE S10 — healthcare access for labour migrants
Publisher: Pharos
Landing page: https://www.pharos.nl/kennisbank/zorgen-voor-arbeidsmigranten/
Report URL: https://www.pharos.nl/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Zorg-en-voor-arbeidsmigranten-rapport-Pharos.pdf
Fetch date: 2026-09-03

Excerpt:
Pharos estimates that more than 375,000 Central and Eastern European labour migrants
were in the Netherlands. Its exploratory study used 10 worker interviews, 18 professional
interviews, a questionnaire and a session with about 35 experts. Seventy-five per cent
of surveyed/interviewed workers reported good health on arrival. Barriers to care include
language, cost, lack of time, distance/transport and unfamiliarity with the Dutch system.
Employers often arrange insurance and housing. Dental care is not routine and cost is a
barrier. Physical work/long days and mental stress create health risks.

Limit: small, non-representative exploratory sample; the “more than 375,000” estimate
is not a count of the four language/origin groups in this study.

Excerpt:
Westland estimates that 12,000 labour migrants work in the municipality, about 4,500

SOURCE S13 — Venlo labour migrants and service infrastructure
Publisher: Municipality of Venlo / Venlo municipal council
Report URL: https://gemeenteraad.venlo.nl/Documenten/130-RIB-bijlage-1.pdf
Current information page: https://www.venlo.nl/informatie-en-adviespunten
WorkInNL update: https://nu.venlo.nl/nieuws/gemeenten-noord-limburg-openen-zes-Work-nl-informatiepunten-voor-eu-arbeidsmigranten
Fetch date: 2026-09-03

Excerpt:
The municipal report estimated 8,200 labour migrants worked in Venlo in 2020; three
in five stayed less than a year and the estimated short-stay-bed shortage was 3,000.
It identified language, communication and care as issues. Current MyInfoNL/WorkInNL
provision gives free information about housing, work, care, rights and language in up
to 16 languages; six North Limburg walk-in points opened in 2026.

Limit: the worker and bed figures are from 2020. The free public service is evidence
of need but also a direct substitute for a paid information-only offer.

# Research — diaspora demand in the Netherlands (folder drafts/research/diaspora-demand)
Question: which physical or licensed services and goods do the Polish, Ukrainian, Russian-speaking and Belarusian communities in the Netherlands buy from providers who speak their language, and which of those are under-served in the Randstad (Amsterdam, Zaanstad, Haarlem, Utrecht, Rotterdam, The Hague) and around the horticulture and logistics belts (Westland, Venlo, Waalwijk)?
Sources to use: CBS StatLine population by origin and municipality (Polish, Ukrainian, Russian, Belarusian), CBS and KVK statistics on migrant entrepreneurs, the Roemer advisory committee report on labour migrants (2020) and follow-ups, IND/Ukrainian temporary-protection figures, Dutch municipal reports on Polish communities (e.g. Westland, Zaanstad, The Hague), trade-body and NGO reports; a structured survey of existing Polish- or Russian-language providers in NL by category (accounting and tax, driving schools, real-estate agents, dentists and medical intake, childcare, legal and sworn translation, food retail and wholesale, construction and installation crews, car repair, funeral, insurance advice, employment agencies) built from public directories and company sites. Record for each category: number of visible providers, geography, whether Dutch licensing is required, and evidence of demand (waiting lists, prices, community-forum complaints).

- Final reply ≤200 words: the ranked candidates and the honest paragraph.

# Research — the boring-business lens: which small physical businesses in the Netherlands quietly last (folder drafts/research/boring-business)
Question: the other instruments asked what is new. This one asks what does not die. Using CBS (StatLine) and KVK data on firm survival, exits and bankruptcies by sector and size class (two to ten staff), plus margin and income data where published (CBS financiën van ondernemingen, sector monitors from Rabobank/ING/ABN AMRO, branch bodies), identify the physical, non-IT sectors in the Netherlands where small firms have the highest five- and ten-year survival, stable or rising demand, and owner incomes above the median, and where the owner population is ageing (CBS ondernemers by age, KVK succession data), so that acquisition or entry is realistic. Then cross that list with what is actually for sale: Brookz, Bedrijventekoop.nl, MKB-adviseurs listings, sector-specific brokers (count listings, asking multiples where stated, typical revenue bands). Sectors to test at minimum: installation trades (electrical, HVAC, plumbing), maintenance and inspection services, industrial cleaning and specialised cleaning, pest control, logistics niches (last-mile, cold chain, ADR), vehicle services, laundries, equipment rental, waste and recycling niches, B2B supply and wholesale, food production niches, funeral services, storage. For each surviving sector: the survival and income figures with source, the licence or certification needed and whether its exam is Dutch-only, the AI gate (does automation or AI touch the demand or only the back office), what the buyer of a firm actually needs to run it (Dutch customer contact, trade skill, a licence held by a named person), and whether a EUR 100k buyer with bank finance is realistic (typical deal sizes, Qredits/bank SME acquisition finance conditions). Sources: CBS StatLine (bedrijfsdemografie, overlevingskansen, faillissementen, financiën), KVK (bedrijvendynamiek, overdracht), Brookz overnamebarometer, bank sector monitors, branch bodies, Qredits/NVB.

Do not re-propose the seven candidates already kept in `drafts/notes/2026-09-03-fable-triage.md` (read it first); you may report where this instrument strengthens or weakens one of them.

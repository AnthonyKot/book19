# Dutch licences and certificates obtainable within twelve months

Research cut-off: 3 September 2026. Candidate: age 40; English, Russian and Polish; A2 Dutch; EUR 100,000; regulatory literacy; engineering habit; 30,000 LinkedIn audience. “A2 pass” below means the work can be delivered in English with A2 Dutch, or a Dutch-speaking partner can own the customer interface. It does **not** waive a personal Dutch-language or technical requirement.

Each records URL, publisher, fetch date and relied-on text or data size.

Important limits:

- UWV evidence is mainly an occupational “promising” classification, not vacancy counts, prices, founder income or proof that a new firm can win customers. Its strongest quantified context is that 144 occupations were structurally promising and 79 of 190 technical occupations (42%) met that test. [S01]
- Provider prices are current examples, not regulated market averages. VAT treatment differs. Equipment, vehicles, premises, insurance, lost earnings and failed attempts are excluded unless explicitly shown.
- “No English exam found” means no official/exam-provider offer was located in this run; it is not proof that an accommodation can never exist.
- Twelve months means administratively possible for a qualified entrant, not guaranteed. Prior experience, examination capacity, VOG/medical checks, audits and municipal processing can be decisive.
- F-gas is in a 2026 transition and EP rules refresh annually. Obtain written eligibility and a fixed quote before spending.
- AI gates are reasoned 2030 directional judgments. Where UWV directly attributes declining translation demand to AI, that is identified as evidence; elsewhere the judgment is an inference from how much of the paid work is physical, local, auditable and automatable.

## Ranked licence-plus-business candidates

### 1. English-certified F-gas heat-pump service company

Obtain BRL 200 A1 in English, then operate inside an existing BRL 100 firm first; use the EUR 100,000 later to acquire or certify a small service company. UWV calls installation labour tight to very tight and explicitly names heat-pump mechanics; the wider technical benchmark is 79 of 190 occupations structurally promising. Edge: engineering habit, regulatory literacy, capital and audience-led recruiting. **A2 gate: pass** with a Dutch customer owner. **AI gate: pass**: diagnosis improves, but refrigerant handling, leak integrity and accountable field work remain. First paid test: one subcontracted maintenance day under a BRL 100-certified partner after personal certification. **Kill fact:** the English A1 provider expects refrigeration theory, several years' experience and brazing; if an entry assessment rejects the candidate, this is an acquisition/hiring thesis, not a personal route. [S01][S04]

### 2. EP-W energy-label and retrofit-evidence boutique

Build through a Dutch-qualified EP-W/B adviser and certified umbrella, then decide whether to obtain own BRL 9500 certification. UWV explicitly names energy-performance advisers; RVO says training averages 4–6 days, while a substantial provider route is 8 weeks and four Cito modules cost EUR 1,579.55 ex VAT. Edge: regulatory literacy, engineering discipline, EUR 100,000 hiring capacity and a large audience for landlord/homeowner acquisition. **A2 gate: conditional pass**: the founder cannot presently sit the Dutch route; the qualified adviser signs and the partner owns Dutch customers. **AI gate: pass/mixed**: reporting compresses, site evidence and regulated accountability persist. First paid test: pre-sell five diaspora-landlord labels delivered through an umbrella. **Kill fact:** contribution margin after umbrella/audit/rework cost is too small to fund customer acquisition and a qualified adviser. [S15]

### 3. Drone inspection and evidence service

Treat A1/A3+A2 as cheap access, not the product; sell repeatable roof, solar, construction-progress or insurance evidence, and buy STS only when a signed job requires it. Open-category training is EUR 199 inc VAT, RDW certificate EUR 10 and operator number EUR 23. There is no UWV shortage signal; the official commissioned forecast rises from 2,460 drones in 2019 to 11,230 in 2050 but is explicitly uncertain. Edge: engineering workflows, multilingual reports, regulatory literacy and 30,000-person distribution. **A2 gate: pass. AI gate: pass/mixed**: autonomous flight commoditises pilots but expands demand for trustworthy analysis. First paid test: fixed-price roof/solar report for one property manager within Open rules. **Kill fact:** ten buyer interviews reveal willingness to pay only for commodity photos, not the evidence/report. [S08]

### 4. Multilingual industrial electrical-safety service

Combine English NEN 3140 VP and VOL-VCA with calibrated testing and a genuinely qualified technical partner; progress to InstallQ only when customer contracts justify it. NEN training is 3.5 hours/EUR 275 ex VAT and VOL-VCA one-day course plus exam starts at EUR 348 ex VAT, but neither alone authorises independent electrical contracting. Demand support is the UWV installation shortage and the 79/190 structurally promising technical occupations. Edge: engineering habit, Polish/Russian workforce access and regulatory documentation. **A2 gate: conditional pass** with Dutch client lead. **AI gate: pass**: AI can draft records, not perform measurements or carry liability. First paid test: a customer-appointed portable-equipment inventory/inspection day under the partner's competence system. **Kill fact:** target buyers require SCIOS Scope 8 or MBO-4 credentials rather than accepting the proposed NEN/partner structure. [S01][S02][S03]

### 5. English TCVT mobile-crane operator-to-contracting wedge

The W4-01 route is unusually concrete: an English eight-day course/exam costs EUR 4,750 plus EUR 195 ex VAT registration, and the credential is statutory for covered mobile-crane work. UWV's promising lists support crane/mobile-equipment work, though no vacancy count was found. Start as an employed or agency operator; only later consider crew supply—EUR 100,000 is not a sensible new-crane budget. Edge: engineering mindset, multilingual site coordination and recruiting reach. **A2 gate: pass conditionally** if the site's safety language is supported. **AI gate: pass/mixed** through 2030: assistance/remote control grow, but changing worksites retain accountable operators. First paid test: obtain written conditional interest from three agencies before enrolling. **Kill fact:** providers or employers require prior machine hours that cannot be acquired safely through the course/stage path. [S14]

### 6. Acquire a certified asbestos-survey microbusiness

Do not pitch this as a personal DIA shortcut: the exam requires Dutch and DIA may work only for a certified survey company. Instead, use capital and regulatory/process skill to acquire or fund a company with a retained Dutch-speaking DIA. Evidence is narrower but tangible: UWV lists asbestos removal regionally, one firm seeks freelance DIAs for regular work in Eindhoven, Arnhem and Nijmegen, and the modelled incumbent pool contains 761 DIAs. Edge: compliance operations, engineering discipline, capital and multilingual workforce sourcing. **A2 gate: conditional business pass; personal fail. AI gate: pass** because field sampling, chain of custody and signed surveys persist. First paid test: agree one overflow survey through a process-certified partner and employed DIA before any acquisition. **Kill fact:** no certified DIA will stay through change of control on economics that fit EUR 100,000. [S09]

## Did this instrument find anything a Dutch founder could not? (honest, 91 words)

No exclusive regulatory arbitrage appeared. A Dutch founder can read the same registers, hire the same certificate holders and see the same UWV lists. The non-obvious value is comparative: English F-gas exams only arrived in August 2026; English TCVT is possible; NEN 3140, VCA, forklift, HACCP and leisure boating are often mistaken for stronger permissions than they are; and EP, InstallQ, asbestos and childcare shift the real moat from a personal badge to a controlled company. This reader's distinctive advantage is therefore not licence access. It is financing, recruiting and distributing a multilingual, audit-ready operator around scarce Dutch-qualified people.

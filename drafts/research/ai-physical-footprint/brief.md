# Research — the physical footprint of AI in the Netherlands, 2026–2030 (folder drafts/research/ai-physical-footprint)
Question: the other instruments asked what AI takes away. This one asks what it builds. Which physical, on-site or licensed work in the Netherlands does the AI build-out itself buy, and which of it could a small new operator sell outside IT? Scope: data-centre construction, fit-out and operation (Amsterdam/Haarlemmermeer/Schiphol-Rijk, Hollands Kroon/Middenmeer, Groningen/Eemshaven, Zeewolde/Flevoland, the national "hyperscale" policy and municipal moratoria); grid connection and congestion (TenneT, Liander, Stedin congestion maps, waiting lists, transformer and substation programmes, battery storage permits); cooling, HVAC and water (heat re-use obligations, drinking-water disputes, cooling technicians); backup power and fuel (generators, UPS, diesel/HVO logistics, testing regimes); fibre and civil works; physical security, guarding and access control (ISO 27001 physical controls, Wpbr licence for security firms); commissioning, testing and inspection (SCIOS, NEN 3140, thermography, electrical inspections); decommissioning and e-waste (WEEELABEX, ITAD, data-bearing-device destruction certificates); staff housing and site logistics for construction crews. For each: evidence that demand is rising with dates (permits, tenders, job postings by operators and contractors, trade-body statements, UWV kansrijke beroepen), who the buyers are (hyperscalers, colocation operators, general contractors, municipalities), the licence or certification the operator needs and whether its exam is Dutch-only, the incumbents, and where a two-to-ten-person firm can enter as subcontractor or specialist. Sources: TenneT/Liander/Stedin, Dutch Data Center Association, RVO, Rijksoverheid, municipal permit registers and council decisions, tender platforms (TenderNed), operator and contractor job boards, UWV, trade bodies (Techniek Nederland, Bouwend Nederland).

Do not re-propose the seven candidates already kept in `drafts/notes/2026-09-03-fable-triage.md` (read it first); you may report where this instrument strengthens or weakens one of them.

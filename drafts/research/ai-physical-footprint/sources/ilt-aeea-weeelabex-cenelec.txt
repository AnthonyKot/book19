Title: Wet- en regelgeving AEEA

Key excerpts:
- The Regeling afgedankte elektrische en elektronische apparatuur governs processors of separately collected AEEA.
- Processors must hold a WEEELABEX conformity declaration and demonstrate compliance with the CENELEC 50625 standard ("de kwaliteit van verwerking of het omgaan met schadelijke stoffen").
- The declaration is company- and location-specific and per WEEE stream. Type 0 processing (manual handling without depollution) needs no certificate.
- Depollution, motor dismantling, component removal and shredding require certification; repair and refurbishment, "including hard drive replacement", fall outside certification scope.
- Mentions "harde schijven of andere onderdelen uit afgedankte computers" often needing certified destruction for security reasons; no detailed ICT-specific framework.

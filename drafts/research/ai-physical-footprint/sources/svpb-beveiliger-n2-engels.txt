Title: Beveiliger N2 (crebo 25690)

Key excerpts:
- SVPB "examineert het beroepsgerichte Engels voor Beveiliger (niveau 2) binnen het examenonderdeel Doelmatig optreden basis", at A2 level (listening, speaking, conversation); aim that candidates can "redden" in English.
- The exam pages and materials are entirely in Dutch; the Beveiliger N2 diploma is an mbo-2 qualification. The page does not state a Dutch language level requirement and does not offer an English-language version of the exam.
- Beroepsgericht Engels (per search summary of the SVPB page): 13 assignments, 30 points; no text-to-speech.
Limits: SVPB does not explicitly say "Dutch-only", but no non-Dutch exam route is published. Treat the Beveiliger diploma as Dutch-language.

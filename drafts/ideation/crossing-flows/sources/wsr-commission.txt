Regulation (EU) 2024/1157 "entered into force on 20 May 2024"; "most provisions apply from 21 May 2026 and
most export rules will apply from 21 May 2027". From 21 May 2027 "the export of non-hazardous waste, also
known as 'green-listed' waste, will generally be prohibited to non-OECD countries" unless the country has
notified the Commission and demonstrated environmentally sound management (Annexes VIII and IX). Plastic
waste exports to non-OECD countries banned from 21 November 2026. Procedures electronic via a central
system (DIWASS) from 21 May 2026. Page says nothing about used goods / proof of functionality.

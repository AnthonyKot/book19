# Seeds for the ideation metaprompt (scripts/prompts/idea-metaprompt.md)

Run with: scripts/ideate.sh <slug> "<seed>". One seed per agent; seeds must not overlap.

## assembled-machines (agent A, 2026-09-04)
Things Dutch small and mid-sized firms buy as equipment and that become a regulated machine, installation
or product the moment they are assembled, installed, modified, connected or put on the EU market: on
factory floors, in workshops, in warehouses, in greenhouses, on building sites, in vehicles. Think of the
moment a bought thing turns into something someone must sign for.

## crossing-flows (agent B, 2026-09-04)
Physical things and people that move between the Netherlands and Poland, Ukraine and the wider
Russian-speaking world in either direction, and that need a file to be legal on arrival: goods, used
equipment, materials, vehicles, crews, products sold to backers or buyers abroad. Think of the moment
a border turns a shipment or a person into paperwork someone must own.

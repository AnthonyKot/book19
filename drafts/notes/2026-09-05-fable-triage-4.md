## Added to the kept list

- **NEW #13. The spray crew's file.** Directive (EU) 2024/869 on lead and diisocyanates, transposed
  and in force in the Netherlands from 9 April 2026 with transitional values to 31 December 2028
  (verified twice), plus the REACH training duty for diisocyanate users in force since August 2023
  (verified). Unit: the exposure survey, register and training record for a spray-foam, PU-coating
  or body-shop crew, sold per crew per year; the lead file for blasting and demolition crews as the
  second line. Precedents: Synergy Environmental Solutions (Stoke-on-Trent, 2012) and EHS Analytical
  Solutions (San Diego), both re-traced. The review's amendment is why it ranks last of the kept:
  the Labour Inspectorate expects the assessor to work at occupational-hygienist level, so the
  hygienist partner moves from step two to the first sale, and near the limit values multi-day
  measurement is expected rather than modelling. Kept because the crews are the reader's, the rule
  is dated and the precedent is a one-engineer firm; ranked low because the Dutch gate is a
  partner, not a paper.

## Watch list additions (one fact from entry)

  twenty spaces need charging by 1 January 2027 (verified). Precedent AMP Renewables (UK,

  building decree; installers and grid consultants own the market if there is no exemption gap.
- **The roof file.** Net metering ends 1 January 2027 and the solar mandate for new buildings

  from €1,500) is real, but in the Netherlands the insurers' Scope 12 inspection regime already
  occupies the seat. Watch until one Dutch SME owner says what they cannot buy.
- **The extraction file** (local exhaust ventilation test per system; UK precedent from £250 a
  system) and **the mask file** (fit test per face): no Dutch rule creates the record outside
  asbestos; both live under British guidance. Watch, as step-three iron for #13.

diminishing return is also visible. Seed C was, in the agent's own words, "largely pre-harvested

insurers already sell the paper. The pool is close to complete for this reader's edge. The next
step is not a fifth seed; it is the pitches, and the author's four constraints.

# Pitch 8 — The used-equipment inspection and the not-waste file (Family A)

**The unit and the file.** For one lot of used Dutch machinery, workshop equipment or dismantled
greenhouse kit sold to a buyer in Poland or Ukraine: a functionality test and record per unit,
the used-goods declaration that keeps the shipment goods rather than waste, and an inspection
report in the buyer's language before payment. Per lot, estimate €700–1,500; the German
precedent charges from €720 a machine.

**The rule and the date.** Waste Shipment Regulation (EU) 2024/1157, applying from 21 May 2026
(verified), requires the per-lot functionality record; the Dutch inspectorate's per-device test,
label and declaration for electrical equipment verified (crossing-flows review); the position of
non-electrical machinery still unverified.

**Who buys.** Two payers: Dutch dealers and growers selling east who need the file to ship, and
Polish and Ukrainian buyers who want an inspection in their language before wiring money. The
greenhouse line the author raised sits here: Dutch glasshouses are rebuilt on a cycle and the
screens, boilers, irrigation and lighting go east.

**Why you.** Engineering habit for the test protocol and the record; Polish, Ukrainian and Russian
for the buyer's report; regulatory literacy for the waste-versus-goods line. No Dutch exam. The
sanctions line is drawn at Russia and Belarus, hard.

**The precedent.** Mevas, Germany, since 2006, 700-plus inspections a year, from €720 a machine
(verified), with the payer switched to the CEE buyer as the reader's variant.

**The ninety-day test.** One paid inspection with a Ukrainian-language report for one buyer,
found through the reader's network. Under €300.

**Steps two and three.** Step two: standing agreements with two Dutch dealers and one greenhouse
demolition contractor. Step three: the trade itself, buying and reselling lots with the file
attached, on consignment first.

**The AI line.** A machine is tested by hand on a site; AI writes the report faster and reads the
regulation, both of which are the reader's margin.

**The kill fact.** Dutch dealers already ship with a used-goods opinion the buyers accept, or the
buyers will not pay before seeing the machine. Two dealer calls and two buyer calls.

**The number to settle first.** What the Dutch dealer association's used-goods opinion costs and
whether the inspectorate accepts it for the new regime.

**Verdict under the four assumptions.** Ranked eighth: capital-light, beside a job, no
retraining, hands-on and on site. Rises if the greenhouse line proves large; it is the most
natural fit with the reader's network of the family-A pitches.

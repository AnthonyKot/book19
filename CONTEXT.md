# Book 19 — The Third Engine (working title) — CONTEXT

## 1. Thesis
Books 4 and 18 covered firms as going concerns and an IT-adjacent second business. Book 19 asks what the
same reader can earn outside software that survives AI progress to 2030. Working thesis after the research
phase: no instrument found a secret market; the reader's edge is languages plus regulatory literacy plus
capital applied to physical work that Dutch operators find bureaucratic and foreign makers find foreign.

## 2. Reader and fixed assumptions
The book 18 reader: ~40, enterprise developer, Zaandam, Dutch A2, English/Russian/Polish, ~EUR 100k, four
books of regulatory reading, engineering habit, ~30k LinkedIn audience. Draft edge inventory in
`drafts/edge-inventory.md` (published as a memo) awaits the author's correction. Four constraints still
open: passive vs hands-on; alongside the job or replacing it; capital ceiling; retraining tolerance.

## 3. Method
Book 18 spine (demand → payment → economics → operations → resilience; scalability test; verdicts TEST NOW /
TEST THROUGH A PARTNER / WATCH / ACQUIRE, DO NOT BUILD / DO NOT ENTER) plus two gates: the A2 gate and the
AI gate. Research instruments run by agents from public sources with saved excerpts; each memo ends with an
honesty paragraph. Essays (when written) follow book 18's TEMPLATE plus a "Why you" section.

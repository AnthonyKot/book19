## Excerpt & Findings

- **Platform Growth & Volume:**
  - Annual raised capital:
    - 2023: NT$ 2.7 billion (~$85M USD)
    - 2024: NT$ 2.9 billion (~$91M USD)
    - 2025: NT$ 3.5 billion (~$110M USD) — all-time record.
  - In May 2026, cumulative platform volume surpassed **NT$ 20 billion**.
  - Project distribution shifted to a healthy trapezoid structure:
    - Projects raising > NT$ 10,000,000 (~$315k USD): 60 projects in 2023, rising to 97 projects in 2025.
    - Projects raising > NT$ 1,000,000 (~$31.5k USD): 391 in 2023 to 451 in 2025.
    - Average project funding increased from NT$ 1.77M (2023) to NT$ 2.34M (2025).
- **Representative Top Physical/Hardware Campaigns (2023–2025):**
  - **iMini F1 Hidden Motorcycle Helmet Bluetooth Speaker (iMini F1 隱藏式安全帽藍牙喇叭):** Raised **NT$ 16,000,000+** (~$500k USD) with over 4,500 backers. Low-profile Bluetooth 5.3 intercom/speaker designed for scooter/motorcycle commuters.
  - **Allround Desk Motorized Standing Desk (鷗圓電動升降桌):** Raised **NT$ 16,000,000+** (~$500k USD).
  - **World's Smallest Fingertip Pump (世界最小指尖幫浦 - Aerogogo / local brand):** Raised **NT$ 12,440,000** (~$390k USD) in 2025.
  - **3E Island Quick Lock (3E 小島快鎖):** Raised **NT$ 10,000,000+** (~$315k USD) in 2023. Smart door lock engineered for humid subtropical environments.
- **Regulatory Context:** Taiwanese domestic consumer products comply with BSMI (Bureau of Standards, Metrology and Inspection) and NCC (for wireless/RF). Compliance with EU CE, WEEE, and CRA is rarely done by local SMEs.

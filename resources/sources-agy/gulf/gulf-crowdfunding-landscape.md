# Source Excerpt: Gulf Region Crowdfunding Landscape & Zoomaal Status

- **URL:** https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/59489-77 (PitchBook Profile: Zoomaal) & https://www.kenresearch.com/banking-financial-services-and-insurance/financial-services/middle-east-crowdfunding-market/515147-93.html (Ken Research: Middle East Crowdfunding Market Outlook)
- **Fetch Date:** 2026-09-03
- **Topic:** Reward-based crowdfunding vs. equity/debt platforms in the GCC/MENA region

## Findings

1. **Zoomaal Status:**
   - Zoomaal (launched 2012 in Beirut/Dubai as the Arab world's flagship reward-crowdfunding platform) is officially listed as **out of business / inactive**.
   - No major reward-based physical product campaign has cleared the platform in 2023–2026.

2. **Regional Alternative Finance Structure:**
   - The GCC/MENA crowdfunding ecosystem is overwhelmingly structured around **debt (P2P lending) and equity crowdfunding**, regulated by regional monetary authorities (e.g., UAE DFSA/FSRA, Saudi Capital Market Authority - CMA).
   - Prominent operational platforms in 2023–2026:
     - **Eureeca:** Regulated cross-border equity crowdfunding (UAE/UK).
     - **Beehive:** P2P debt / invoice financing for SMEs (UAE/Saudi).
     - **Manafa & Scopeer:** Saudi CMA-regulated debt/equity crowdfunding platforms.
   - Reward-based hardware/physical-product crowdfunding at scale (comparable to Kickstarter/Indiegogo) does **not** exist in the Gulf region. Consumer tech/hardware makers from the region launch on Kickstarter US/UK or Indiegogo rather than domestic platforms.

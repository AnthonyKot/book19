- **Fetch Date:** 2026-09-03
- **Topic:** JD.com Crowdfunding Platform Status 2022–2026

## Excerpt & Findings

- On September 30, 2022, JD.com announced that its crowdfunding portal (京东众筹) would cease business operations.
- The entrance to JD Crowdfunding was closed on September 30, 2022 at 10:00, and all official operations terminated on October 10, 2022.
- Pre-existing active campaigns were fulfilled through normal logistics, but no new reward crowdfunding projects have been hosted since Q4 2022 through 2026.
- Strategic context: JD pivoted its financial and retail resources toward core e-commerce, supply chain finance, and consumer credit (Baitiao), exiting reward crowdfunding due to tightened financial regulations and changing consumer behaviors in mainland China.
- Conclusion for 2023–2026: Zero active physical reward-crowdfunding volume on JD Crowdfunding.

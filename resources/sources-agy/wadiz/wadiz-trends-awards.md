## Excerpt & Findings

- **Platform Structure:** Wadiz is South Korea's largest reward and pre-order crowdfunding platform.
  - In the Wadiz Awards 2023 and 2024, the top two dominant categories were **Tech/Home Appliances (20%)** and **Fashion/Apparel (20%)**, followed by Beauty (19%).
  - Average pledge per backer in Tech/Appliances grew from 120,000 KRW (2023) to 140,000 KRW (2024) and 150,000 KRW (2025).
- **Representative Top Physical/Hardware Campaigns (2023–2025):**
  - **Hanker - ThermaThera (써마쎄라):** High-frequency RF home beauty & skin lifting device. Cumulative funding reached **2.6 billion KRW** (~$1.95M USD).
  - **JUST (저스):** High-lumen ultra-compact smart home beam projector. Cumulative funding **1.7 billion KRW** (~$1.28M USD).
  - **Shen Korea - Stiluister Air Gun (슈틸루스터):** High-pressure brushless motor portable air duster/blower. Cumulative funding **1.7 billion KRW** (~$1.28M USD).
  - **Mune / Magmo Pro (아이폰 맥세이프 통화 녹음기):** MagSafe snap-on hardware iPhone call recorder utilizing piezoelectric sensor to record voice vibrations directly from the chassis. Wadiz campaign raised **440 million KRW** (~$330k USD) with > 3,800 backers.
- **Regulatory Context:** Korean domestic hardware adheres to KC (Korea Certification) certification. Products rarely pursue EU CE, RED, or WEEE registration unless backed by large conglomerates (Samsung, LG), creating an export friction barrier.

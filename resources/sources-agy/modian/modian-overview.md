- **Topic:** Modian Crowdfunding 2023–2026 Categories and Scale

## Excerpt & Findings

- Modian is China's primary surviving creative reward-crowdfunding platform.
- Category Breakdown:
  - Strongly dominated by ACGN (Anime, Comics, Gaming, Novelty): indie video games, tabletop games / TCG cards, art toys / blind boxes, model kits, and cultural publications (e.g. Chinese National Geography collaborations).
  - "Tech / Hardware" is a marginal category on Modian (<5% of platform transaction volume). Almost all multi-million RMB campaigns are IP-driven collectibles or board games (e.g., licensed figurines, commemorative coins, illustration books).
  - Chinese hardware innovators bypass domestic reward crowdfunding (after JD Crowdfunding and Taobao Crowdfunding declined) and launch hardware directly on Kickstarter / Indiegogo for global markets or via standard pre-orders on JD/Tmall/Douyin.
- Platform volume: Leading IP projects routinely raise RMB 1M–10M (~$140k–$1.4M), but physical utility/electronic consumer hardware without IP affiliation is virtually absent.

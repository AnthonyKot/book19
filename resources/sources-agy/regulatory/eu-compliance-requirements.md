# EU Regulatory Compliance Landscape for Physical & Connected Products (2023–2026)

- **Date of compilation:** 2026-09-03
- **Context:** Book 18/19 regulatory distribution partner role based in the Netherlands (NL).

## 1. General Product Safety & Economic Operator Requirement
- **GPSR (General Product Safety Regulation, (EU) 2023/988):**
  - In force / mandatory since **13 December 2024**.
  - Any consumer product placed on the EU market (including online sales via Amazon, marketplaces, or direct-to-consumer) must have an **Economic Operator established in the EU** (manufacturer, importer, or authorized representative) responsible for compliance documentation, safety warnings in official member-state languages, and contact details displayed on the product/packaging.
  - Absence of an EU economic operator blocks customs clearance and triggers automated delisting on Amazon.de/.nl.

## 2. Electrical, Radio & Connected Hardware CE Directives
- **RED (Radio Equipment Directive 2014/53/EU):**
  - Applies to any product with RF capabilities (Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Zigbee, Cellular, proprietary RF).
  - Articles 3.1(a) Health & Safety (EN 62368-1), 3.1(b) EMC (EN 301 489 series), 3.2 Radio Spectrum (EN 300 328).
  - Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30 (RED cybersecurity / network protection) enforceable before full CRA transition.
- **LVD (Low Voltage Directive 2014/35/EU):** Electrical safety for equipment operating with voltage rating between 50 and 1000 V AC / 75 and 1500 V DC. (Low-voltage USB devices <50V AC/75V DC fall under GPSR/EMC/RED).
- **EMC (Electromagnetic Compatibility Directive 2014/30/EU):** Emissions and immunity standards (EN 55032, EN 55035).
- **RoHS Directive (2011/65/EU & Delegated Directive 2015/863):** Restriction of 10 hazardous substances (lead, cadmium, phthalates DEHP/BBP/DBP/DIBP, etc.) in electrical equipment. Requires technical file and supplier declarations.

## 3. Cyber Resilience Act (CRA - Regulation (EU) 2024/2847)
- Adopted October 2024, entered into force late 2024; staged application reaching full enforcement by 2026/2027.
- Applies to all **Products with Digital Elements (PDE)** — connected hardware, smart home sensors, IoT gadgets, motorized smart desks, companion apps.
- Core mandates:
  - Security by design, vulnerability handling process, free security updates for the expected product lifetime (or min 5 years).
  - Mandatory incident/actively exploited vulnerability reporting within 24 hours to EU CSIRTs and ENISA.
  - Mandatory CE marking integration: non-compliant PDE cannot bear the CE mark.
  - Severe barrier for Asian/US crowdfunding creators who lack EU-based compliance infrastructure.

## 4. Circular Economy, Battery & Packaging EPR
- **EU Battery Regulation ((EU) 2023/1542):**
  - Fully in effect across 2024–2026.
  - Requirements for portable batteries: removable/replaceable by end users, strict labeling (chemical symbols, QR code), CE marking conformity, and battery EPR registration in each destination member state (e.g., Stibat in the Netherlands).
- **Packaging EPR (Extended Producer Responsibility):**
  - Mandatory in every EU country where goods are delivered.
  - Netherlands: Afvalfonds Verpakkingen / Verpact.
  - Germany: Verpackungsgesetz (LUCID registration + dual system contract like Interseroh/Der Grüne Punkt).
  - France: Citeo / ADEME UIN.
- **WEEE Directive (2012/19/EU):**
  - Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment registration.
  - Netherlands: Stichting OPEN (Wecycle registration and reporting).
  - Germany: Stiftung EAR.

## 5. Value Proposition for a Netherlands-based Distributor
- Non-EU creators (US, Japan, Korea, Taiwan) see EU compliance as an impenetrable, fragmented bureaucracy (27 member states, separate WEEE/packaging registries, language packaging requirements, GPSR responsible person liability, RED lab testing).
- A Dutch B.V. acts as the **Importer of Record / Authorized Representative**, assumes the regulatory file, registers with Verpact/Stichting OPEN, arranges Notified Body or accredited lab verification, and opens distribution into EU Amazon (.de, .nl, .fr) and retail channels (Coolblue, Bol.com, MediaMarkt).

## Excerpt & Findings

- **Platform Structure:** Makuake is Japan's premier "Ouen Kounyu" (support purchase) platform. Annual project volume exceeds 6,500–7,000 campaigns. Gadgets, home appliances, outdoor goods, and specialized craftsmanship represent the highest funding tiers.
- **Makuake Award 2023 (Gold Award):**
  - Project: "超硬合金包丁 KISEKI:" (Fukuda Cutter / Fukuda Hamono Kogyo, Seki City, Gifu Prefecture). World's first mass-produced cemented carbide kitchen knife. Raised > JPY 40M across campaign phases.
- **Makuake Award 2024 (Award of the Year / Category Leader):**
  - Project: "WILLCOOK PACKABLE" (Willtex Co., Ltd.). Portable microwave bag using patented flexible conductive heating fabric (HOTOPIA).
  - Performance: Cumulative WILLCOOK series exceeded JPY 48M (~$320k USD) with several thousand supporters. Won CES 2024 Innovation Award. Powered via standard USB-PD power banks (5V/2A to 9V/2A), heats up to 90°C–130°C within minutes for retort food heating and emergency survival.
- **Other Leading Hardware / Physical Categories on Makuake:**
  - Compact induction/heating appliances, ergonomic micro-furniture, emergency prep gadgets ("Phase Free" disaster mitigation gear).
  - High domestic compliance friction (PSE mark for AC power, VCCI/TELEC for RF, Food Sanitation Act for food contact). Japanese makers frequently do not obtain CE/RoHS/WEEE markings, leaving a domestic-only distribution moat.

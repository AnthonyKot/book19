# Makuake awards and 2025 annual hit report

- URLs: https://award.makuake.com/2024/ ; https://www.makuake.co.jp/5102/
- Fetch date: 2026-09-03
- Publisher: Makuake, Inc.

The 2024 award page says awards consider supporter count, total support purchases, communication, public attention and social impact. Named physical products include PLAUD NOTE, a connected AI voice recorder; cado's futon dryer; CIO magnetic silicone cable; a portable microwave bag; VITURE One XR glasses; and HOVERAir flying camera. Awards are editorial, not a complete ranking.

Makuake's 2025 report states that 5,042 projects drew about JPY 15.2 billion and about 1.04 million supporters in 2025. Its annual top five by amount were Kirin future whisky (JPY 273,592,000), il modo Air wallet (JPY 226,212,500), COFO Chair Pro 2 (JPY 208,802,697), RingConn Gen 2 (JPY 142,701,715), and iFLYTEK AINOTE (JPY 129,557,650). The report says the total is the sum of amounts and supporters shown on project pages.

Search-visible project records add these exact threshold-relevant counts: il modo Air carries Makuake's “10,000 supporters achieved” badge; COFO's maker page lists Chair Pro 2 at JPY 208,802,697 and 3,949 supporters; iFLYTEK AINOTE carries the “1,000 supporters achieved” badge. The RingConn page says its separate 2024 Kickstarter raised USD 4.4 million from 18,000 supporters; this is counted only in the Kickstarter data, not twice.

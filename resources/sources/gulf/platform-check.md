The UAE government's current page names Beehive, YallaGive, and Tathmeer, defines the 2022 federal framework in equity terms, and says the central bank regulates loan-based crowdfunding. Saudi Arabia's CMA reports SAR 3.4 billion in securities/debt crowdfunding in 2024 versus SAR 1.5 billion in 2023. SAMA said in April 2024 that it had ten authorized debt-based crowdfunding providers.

Mshrue describes itself as Saudi Arabia's first crowdfunding platform with no debt or equity (i.e. reward-based), but its public landing page provides no funded-volume, backer, project, or category totals. Older Zoomaal sources are not evidence of 2023–2026 scale. Conclusion: a reward platform exists, but measurable Gulf scale in current authoritative sources is overwhelmingly investment/debt rather than reward-product crowdfunding. With no qualifying public product table, the Gulf is not used to manufacture candidates.

Verbatim passage (Article 6(1)):

Operators may mandate an authorised representative to submit the due diligence statement pursuant to Article 4(2) or to submit a simplified declaration pursuant to Article 4a(2) on their behalf. In such cases, the operator shall retain responsibility for the compliance of the relevant product with Article 3.

Verbatim passage:

Operators sourcing relevant products from countries classified as low-risk may conduct simplified due diligence, if they are certain that all of the relevant products were produced in low-risk countries, and have not undergone any mixing with high-risk, standard-risk or unknown origin products.

An operator conducting simplified due diligence must still collect information demonstrating the compliance of its products, but does not need to conduct a full risk assessment or take risk mitigation measures.

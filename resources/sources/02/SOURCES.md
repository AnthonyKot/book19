## Evidence boundary

The pitch's requested number was not available in the required form. The NEa published applications and approvals, but also said some applicants were below the threshold. It did not publish the count above 50 tonnes, the count filed by 31 March 2026, or the count of eligible non-applicants. Its separate “at most 1,500” figure is a planning estimate from historical imports, not a register count (`02-count-boundary`).

The kill fact also remains partly open. Customs Support, Deloitte and Ingdilligenz describe overlapping services, but none publishes a comparable SME price or says supplier-data work is included free with clearance (`02-price-boundary`). No source shows a Dutch SME paying this proposed entrant, nor permission to retain supplier knowledge across clients (`02-payment-boundary`). Official role descriptions do not show a licensed CBAM-consultant credential, but absence in those descriptions is not a statutory opinion (`02-credential-boundary`). These are payment, partner and counsel questions for the ninety-day test.

EUDR is enacted but not yet applicable on the dates stated. The December 2025 amendment is law, not a proposal. Ukraine's low-risk classification and state tools weaken the original wood-file wedge; they do not eliminate the Dutch operator's remaining information and submission duties.

# Wadiz 2024 trend report

- URL: https://makercenter.wadiz.kr/en/board/card/628
- Fetch date: 2026-09-03
- Publisher: Wadiz

The English maker-center page is dated 2024-01-30. It says the report analyzes popular Wadiz projects from January through November 2023 and mixes qualitative and quantitative data. The accessible page describes six trend keywords but does not expose a complete project table with amount and supporter count.

The page's footer explicitly prohibits unauthorized crawling or scraping of reward, maker, story, content, and UI information. Accordingly this research did not crawl Wadiz or treat its editorial examples as an aggregate dataset.

# JD product crowdfunding closure

- URLs: https://www.yicaiglobal.com/news/jdcom-product-crowdfunding-platform-shuts-down ; https://www.ithome.com/0/645/423.htm
- Fetch date: 2026-09-03

Both public reports say JD.com's product-crowdfunding operation stopped on 2022-10-10 and its dedicated entry/site was removed in late September 2022. Existing campaigns were allowed to finish. There can therefore be no JD product-crowdfunding cohort for 2023–2026.

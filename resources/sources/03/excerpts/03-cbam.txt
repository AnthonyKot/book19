“The CBAM definitive regime applies from 1 January 2026.”

“EU importers (or their indirect customs representatives) who import more than the single mass-based threshold of 50 tonnes of CBAM goods into the EU are required to apply for the status of authorised CBAM declarants.”

“What products are under CBAM?
CBAM covers
- iron and steel
- cement
- aluminium
- fertilizers
- electricity
- hydrogen”

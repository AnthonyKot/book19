# Reward-delivery risk

- URLs: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2699251 ; https://repository.upenn.edu/bitstreams/af3b8536-872e-49a9-86b2-8fa37378db34/download
- Fetch date: 2026-09-03
- Author: Ethan R. Mollick, *Delivery Rates on Kickstarter* (2015)

The study used a survey of 47,188 backers. It estimates Kickstarter project-level reward non-delivery at about 9%, plausibly 5%–14%. This is old, Kickstarter-specific evidence, not a 2023–2026 cross-platform rate. It is used only to demonstrate that money pledged and backer count do not prove delivery or repeat retail demand.

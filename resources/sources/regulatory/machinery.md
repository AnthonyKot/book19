# Machinery transition note

- URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1230/en
- Fetch date: 2026-09-03
- Publisher: EUR-Lex

The consolidated Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 lays down health and safety requirements for machinery and related products. Article 54, as corrected, says it applies from 20 January 2027 and repeals Directive 2006/42/EC. A distributor considering CNC machines or other powered machinery therefore needs a product-specific scope analysis against the Directive for placement before that date and the Regulation for placement from that date; merely obtaining a generic “CE certificate” is not the task.

# Indiegogo published data and 2023 roundup

- Dataset index URL: https://webrobots.io/indiegogo-dataset/
- Official roundup URL: https://go.indiegogo.com/blog/2023/08/top-10-crowdfunding-campaigns-of-2023-so-far.html
- Fetch date: 2026-09-03

Web Robots says it launched its Indiegogo collector in May 2016 and ran it monthly. The latest archive it lists is dated 2025-10-13. The downloaded CSV is a discovery snapshot: it has campaign title, category, open/close dates, currency, amount, funding percentage and perks claimed, but does not expose a stable campaign-state field or creator country.

Indiegogo's own August 2023 roundup reports the following first-half amounts: Space Acacia, Travel & Outdoors, USD 1,079,403; AOKZOE A1Pro, Productivity, USD 1,101,051; AYANEO 2S, Innovative Products, USD 1,961,257; GPD WIN Max 2, Innovative Products, USD 2,087,169; AYANEO 2, Innovative Products, USD 2,179,896; and GPD WIN 4, Productivity, USD 3,939,216. It also reports category totals of USD 10,905,278 for Innovative Products and USD 9,053,810 for Productivity. The article does not publish backer counts, so those entries cannot independently pass the two-part screen in this study.

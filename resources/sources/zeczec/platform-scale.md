# Zeczec scale and example

- URLs: https://service.moea.gov.tw/EE514/en/rocpia2/586-7814.html ; https://startup.sme.gov.tw/en/NewsContent/591
- Fetch date: 2026-09-03
- Publishers: Taiwan Ministry of Economic Affairs / Startup Portal Taiwan

The Presidential Innovation Award profile says that, by May 2024, Zeczec had over NT$12.9 billion in one-time pledges, more than NT$140 million in subscriptions, and a cumulative four million backers. It describes about 1,800 proposals annually and says failed campaigns refund pledges.

The Startup Portal article gives one physical-product example: the “Hiding Tou” beverage cup raised NT$38,289,487 against a NT$1,000,000 goal from 31,089 supporters. The article is dated 2023, but it does not give a campaign launch/close date, so the product is not admitted to the dated 2023–2026 candidate set.

No permitted published project-level dataset with close date, amount, supporter count, category, and outcome was found. Zeczec therefore contributes scale context, not platform-wide category or success-rate estimates.

## Evidence boundary

The Stedin notice shows a named end buyer completing procurement, but it does not establish that a two-DIA bureau can win that framework or establish ordinary property-manager pricing. The record does not reveal a completed price for an affordable two-DIA firm, job-level margins, the distribution of DIA certificates among companies, or whether a particular seller and key employees will stay. The recent Advies Lieren/Buro Pear announcement shows a continuity structure, while the register independently confirms both certified entities. It does not disclose transaction form, consideration, payroll, customer retention or post-close economics. It also does not settle how a certification body treats the proposed share transfer and personnel changes. Written certification guidance, partner payment and target documents must settle those points.

The Bbl duty is standing. The August 2026 amendment is enacted and current, but its official explanation says it only updates a standard reference and adds no administrative burden. Neither the rule, certificate counts nor public credit ceilings establish customer budget or lender approval.

How the search works

The reader is fixed: a forty-year-old enterprise developer living in Zaandam, Dutch at A2, English, Russian and Polish at working level, about a hundred thousand euros to put at risk, four books' worth of reading EU and Dutch regulation, an engineering habit, and a professional audience of about thirty thousand. The question is what that person can earn outside software that survives to 2030. The edge inventory states those assumptions so they can be corrected.

Instruments

Rather than brainstorm, the search runs instruments: each is a question the public record can answer, run by an agent that must save every source it relies on and may not invent a number. Five have run so far: crowdfunding platforms as a record of paid demand; the demography and visible supply of the diaspora communities; the businesses on sale because owners retire; the licences an English speaker can obtain within a year; the import flows from Poland and outside the EU; and the regulations that create physical work between now and 2028. Two were run on two different models to see where the models disagree; the comparison is published.

Gates

Every candidate is screened by the Book 18 spine, demand before payment before economics before operations before resilience, and by two gates specific to this reader. The A2 gate: can the business run with A2 Dutch, or does a partner own the Dutch-facing customer? The AI gate: does progress in AI raise or lower demand for this work by 2030? Physical, licensed and local work tends to pass; screen work in disguise tends to fail.

Verdicts

When the essays are written they will carry Book 18's vocabulary: TEST NOW, TEST THROUGH A PARTNER, WATCH, ACQUIRE DO NOT BUILD, DO NOT ENTER. Each will carry one section Book 18 did not have, "Why you": the edge that applies, or the honest admission that a Dutch founder could do the same.

Honesty rules

Regulation is not a budget. A funded campaign is not a retail market. A search that finds no provider is not proof of demand. Every memo ends with a paragraph on whether it found anything a Dutch founder could not; so far none has claimed to. Figures in memos trace to saved excerpts beside them; where a run's claim could not be traced it was cut in the triage.

Built the same way as the eighteen books before it: static pages, a decision record in the repository, and checks that fail the build when a stated count contradicts the files.

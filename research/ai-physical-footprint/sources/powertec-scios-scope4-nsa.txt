Title: Verplichte SCIOS-keuring noodstroomaggregaten (Scope 4 en 7c)

Key excerpts:
- Scope 4 = the combustion installation (diesel engine); Scope 7c = the fuel supply line from external tank to engine.
- Frequencies by thermal input: 20–100 kW every 4 years; over 100 kW every 2 years.
- "Since 1 January 2016 these inspections are mandatory for emergency power generators, as the Activity Decision has applied to emergency power supplies since that date."
- Only SCIOS-certified companies may inspect.
- Generators used only for emergencies at their own site and running fewer than 500 hours a year count as emergency generators with reduced requirements.
Limits: vendor page; the 1 Jan 2026 frequency change mentioned in search summaries (4-year interval for all NSAs under 500 h/yr) was not confirmed on this page.

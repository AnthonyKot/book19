Title: Rapportageplicht energie-efficiëntie datacentra

Key excerpts:
- Legal basis: Energy Efficiency Directive (EED) Article 12, implemented via Dutch regulation published in the Staatscourant/Staatsblad in 2024.
- Threshold: datacenters with "geïnstalleerd IT-vermogen van minimaal 500 kilowatt" must report annually; applies to enterprise, colocation and co-hosting facilities alike.
- Reported metrics: energy efficiency (PUE), water consumption efficiency, waste-heat recovery factor, renewable-energy percentage.
- 2026 reporting window: 16 March – 15 May 2026; annual from 2024 onward.
- Reports go via Mijn RVO to the European Commission and are published on RVO.nl unless marked confidential under the Woo.
- Narrow exemption only for facilities exclusively serving defence or disaster response.
Limits: page does not give the number of reporting datacenters.

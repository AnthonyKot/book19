NEN-EN 529:2005 specifies what a respiratory-protection (ABM) programme should contain; the revised NVvA guideline uses the ABM programme from ISO 16975. Respiratory protection is a supplementary measure when source control is insufficient; for welding fumes, extraction at source first. Outside the asbestos section (Arbobesluit chapter 4 section 5) no statutory fit-test obligation was found in these results; fit testing outside asbestos rests on guidelines (NVvA, ISO 16975-3), not on an article of the Arbobesluit.

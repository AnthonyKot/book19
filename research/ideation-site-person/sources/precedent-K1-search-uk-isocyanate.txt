In the UK isocyanate exposure is measured through air sampling and biological monitoring. COSHH WELs for isocyanates: 0.02 mg/m3 (8h) and 0.07 mg/m3 (15 min). HSE recommends that vehicle spray painters undertake biological monitoring annually. Operator exposure monitoring must be carried out in accordance with HSE method MDHS 25 by a professional occupational hygienist (BOHS). HSE's Health and Safety Laboratory sells the urine isocyanate biological-monitoring assay directly (hsl.gov.uk online ordering). Sysco Environmental sells an "Isocyanates Biological Monitoring Kit".

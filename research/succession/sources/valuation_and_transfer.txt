[V1] Publisher: Brookz
Title: Overname Barometer H1-2026
URL: https://www.brookz.nl/barometer/h1-2026
Published: 2026-08-17. Extracted data: 291 Dutch M&A advisers, covering transactions in businesses with €0.5m–€50m revenue. H1 sale transactions fell 4%; the average reported EBITDA multiple was 5.0. The average for normalized EBITDA of €200,000 was 3.6 versus 7.0 at €10m. In 42% of processes advisers said sellers overvalued the business; the average expectation gap was 23%, and 19% of deals broke. Limitation: adviser survey and mostly larger companies than this listing sample.

[T1] Publisher: KVK
Title: Stappenplan bedrijf overnemen
URL: https://www.kvk.nl/verkopen-en-overnemen/stappenplan-bedrijf-overnemen/
Updated: 2026-07-15. Extracted guidance: nine stages—buyer profile, search, sales memorandum, letter of intent, due diligence, price, financing, contract and KVK registration. The sales memorandum should state history, value, legal form, finances, personnel and sale reason; due diligence verifies figures, contracts and staff obligations.

[T2] Publisher: Brookz
Title: Zo stel je een post-acquisitieplan op
URL: https://www.brookz.nl/kennisbank/bedrijf-kopen/zo-stel-je-een-post-acquisitieplan-op
Extracted guidance: communicate with everyone on day one; use the first 100 days to learn the operation, retain staff and customers and execute a post-acquisition plan. Promptly address employment contracts, trademarks and old receivables; involve employees and communicate continuity to customers and suppliers.

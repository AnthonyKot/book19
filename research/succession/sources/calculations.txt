Reproducible calculations used in findings.md
Prepared: 2026-09-03

Listing sample inputs: sources/listings_sample.csv, 60 rows.
Asking-price buckets use the listed exact price or the upper-bound-safe Bedrijventekoop bands: ≤€100k; €100k–€250k; >€250k–€400k. Counts by sector are produced directly from the CSV. “Quantitative disclosure” excludes “not stated” and “Vertrouwelijk”. Retirement is “yes” only for an explicit pension/age/succession term in the listing narrative.

Qredits debt-service illustration (not a quote or approval): principal P=€250,000, nominal annual fixed rate 9.95%, monthly rate r=0.0995/12, n=60 or 120 payments. Formula: payment=P*r*(1+r)^n/((1+r)^n-1). Result: five years €5,305.61/month (€63,667.35/year); ten years €3,296.85/month (€39,562.20/year). Inputs trace to [F3]. The 1.5% closing fee is €3,750 (=€250,000×0.015). Buyer equity €100,000 plus €250,000 gross loan proceeds equals €350,000 before fee, due diligence, transaction costs and working-capital reserve.

No marketplace asking price was treated as completed transaction value. No missing revenue, profit or headcount was imputed.

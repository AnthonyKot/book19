Artikel 4.15: a list of employees who are or can be exposed to carcinogenic or mutagenic substances (or substances released in a carcinogenic process), noting the exposure undergone. "The retention period for these records has been extended from thirty to forty years"; "Companies are legally required to retain CMR registration at the individual level up to 40 years after the end of exposure" (ITIS, trade summary). Reprotoxic substances brought under the regime by Stb. 2024-127 (Directive 2022/431). Lead: 0.15 to 0.03 mg/m3 from 9 April 2026 under Directive 2024/869. Status: the 40-year retention is verified at trade-summary level, not from the article text; which lead work falls under 4.15 (lead is reprotoxic, not carcinogenic) needs the article text.

TITLE: EP-W/EP-U energy-performance advisers and NOx/SCIOS Scope 6
FETCH DATE: 2026-09-03

SOURCE 1
URL: https://www.rvo.nl/onderwerpen/wetten-en-regels-gebouwen/informatie-epa
PUBLISHER: Netherlands Enterprise Agency (RVO)
EXCERPT (paraphrased): Only a competent EP adviser may prepare energy labels. Training averages four to six days and examinations are offered by Cito, 't Examenpark and SNEI. EP-W/B covers existing housing; EP-W/D adds new housing; EP-U/B covers existing utility buildings and includes W/B competence; EP-U/D has the broadest competence. Advisers must either be BRL 9500-certified or join a certified umbrella organisation. All advisers undertake mandatory annual refresher training, and competent advisers appear in the Centraal Register Techniek.

EXCERPT (paraphrased): SCIOS schemes certify companies and set education and experience requirements for people working within them. SCIOS addresses safety, reliability and environmental impact of combustion/heating, electrical and explosion-safe installations, and company certification operates against an ISO 9001-based scheme.

LIMITS
- No EP examination body found advertising an English examination. All public materials and sample exams found were Dutch; English availability must therefore be recorded as “none found/unconfirmed,” not a categorical legal prohibition.
- “NOx adviser” is not a protected personal profession located in the regulated-professions sources. SCIOS Scope 6 is an emissions-measurement competence within a company-certification system, with experience requirements. Because a defensible current public cost, language and within-12-month novice route were not found, it is not treated as a viable candidate.
- KNMI is a meteorological institute, not a general short licensing route for paid work, and is therefore irrelevant to this screen.

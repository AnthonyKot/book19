Publisher: InstallQ
URL: https://installq.nl/warmtepomp-installaties
Fetch date: 2026-09-03
Excerpt/facts relied on:
- Above-ground heat-pump recognition is a company recognition covering design, calculation,
  installation, inspection, management and maintenance. It requires competence, specified tools,
  standards and documentation and includes a company visit before approval.
- The business can qualify through a Work-Responsible Heat Pump profile, or staff collectively
  holding the Designer/Adviser, First Mechanic service/maintenance and First Mechanic profiles.
  Approved businesses enter EchteInstallateur.nl and the Centraal Register Techniek.

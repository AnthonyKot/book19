Added to the kept list

  • NEW #13. The spray crew's file. Directive (EU) 2024/869 on lead and diisocyanates, transposed and in force in the Netherlands from 9 April 2026 with transitional values to 31 December 2028 (verified twice), plus the REACH training duty for diisocyanate users in force since August 2023 (verified). Unit: the exposure survey, register and training record for a spray-foam, PU-coating or body-shop crew, sold per crew per year; the lead file for blasting and demolition crews as the second line. Precedents: Synergy Environmental Solutions (Stoke-on-Trent, 2012) and EHS Analytical Solutions (San Diego), both re-traced. The review's amendment is why it ranks last of the kept: the Labour Inspectorate expects the assessor to work at occupational-hygienist level, so the hygienist partner moves from step two to the first sale, and near the limit values multi-day measurement is expected rather than modelling. Kept because the crews are the reader's, the rule is dated and the precedent is a one-engineer firm; ranked low because the Dutch gate is a partner, not a paper.

Watch list additions (one fact from entry)

  • The plant energy file. EED Article 11 audits for enterprises above 10 TJ by 11 October 2026 (verified); the Netherlands had not implemented the test at fetch date (verified at RVO). A German engineering office lives on it (DeltaUWB, 2016). Watch, not keep, for the reason the battery and flex packages were cut: Dutch energy consultancy is crowded, and this is engineering consultancy until the implementation names a population the incumbents do not already serve. Leaves watch when the Dutch bill sets the test and CBS gives the count of firms between 10 TJ and the old large-enterprise line.
  • The car-park charging file. EPBD Article 14: non-residential buildings with more than twenty spaces need charging by 1 January 2027 (verified). Precedent AMP Renewables (UK, £1,000–2,500 per charger). Watch until the SME-exemption question is answered for the Dutch building decree; installers and grid consultants own the market if there is no exemption gap.
  • The roof file. Net metering ends 1 January 2027 and the solar mandate for new buildings bites 31 December 2026 (both verified). The German independent-expert precedent (Gutachten Graf, from €1,500) is real, but in the Netherlands the insurers' Scope 12 inspection regime already occupies the seat. Watch until one Dutch SME owner says what they cannot buy.
  • The extraction file (local exhaust ventilation test per system; UK precedent from £250 a system) and the mask file (fit test per face): no Dutch rule creates the record outside asbestos; both live under British guidance. Watch, as step-three iron for #13.

What the round says

The metaprompt keeps producing the shape, and the precedent rule keeps sorting it: five ideas in this round had a named firm elsewhere, and the two without one went straight to watch. The diminishing return is also visible. Seed C was, in the agent's own words, "largely pre-harvested by the triages": housing, agencies, the grid crew, the fleet desk and the inspection microfirms are all a person becoming a file. Seed D found dated rules but in markets where installers and insurers already sell the paper. The pool is close to complete for this reader's edge. The next step is not a fifth seed; it is the pitches, and the author's four constraints.

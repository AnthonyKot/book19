SOURCE S30 — regulated/protected service rules
Publisher: Netherlands Enterprise Agency, Business.gov.nl (official government portal)
Fetch date for all pages: 2026-09-03

ACCOUNTING
URL: https://business.gov.nl/regulations/registration-consultation-accountants-register/
Excerpt: the protected RA and AA accountant titles require registration in the NBA
register; statutory audits require an AFM licence. Ordinary bookkeeping/tax-return help
is outside those protected titles, but providers may not present themselves as RA/AA.

DRIVING INSTRUCTION
URL: https://business.gov.nl/regulations/certification-driving-instructors/
Excerpt: paid driving instructors need a WRM competence card after theory and practical
exams; the card is valid five years. The qualification route/exam uses Dutch.

HEALTHCARE
URL: https://business.gov.nl/regulations/registering-as-healthcare-professional/
Excerpt: doctors and dentists are among the professions requiring BIG registration.
Foreign qualifications must be recognised, sufficient Dutch demonstrated, and periodic
re-registration requirements met. Non-clinical intake/navigation does not permit diagnosis
or treatment and must remain under the clinical provider’s responsibility.

CHILDCARE
Registration URL: https://business.gov.nl/regulations/registration-childcare/
Checklist URL: https://business.gov.nl/starting-your-business/starting-situations/checklist-for-starting-a-childcare-centre/
Law URL: https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0017017/2026-07-01
Excerpt: a childcare centre must apply to the municipality for LRK registration at least
10 weeks before opening and is inspected by the GGD. Staff generally need Dutch at
3F/B2. The multilingual-childcare exception permits English, German or French for no
more than 50% of daily hours—not Polish, Russian, Ukrainian or Belarusian. The statute
contains narrow contextual exceptions, but a mainstream full-day “own-language
childcare” offer does not fit the ordinary multilingual provision and the A2 founder
could not personally fill a Dutch-language pedagogical role. Specialist advice would be
needed before relying on an exception.

LAWYERS AND SWORN TRANSLATORS
Lawyer URL: https://business.gov.nl/regulations/registration-consultation-national-register-lawyers/
Translator URL: https://business.gov.nl/regulations/registration-sworn-interpreters-translators/
Excerpt: practising as an advocaat requires registration, a Dutch law degree and the
three-year professional education route (with EEA recognition routes where applicable).
Sworn translators/interpreters must be in the Rbtv, supply a VOG, meet language/skill
levels (including C1/B2 routes), be sworn by a court and renew after five years. General
legal information or non-sworn translation must not be presented as those protected acts.

FOOD
URL: https://business.gov.nl/regulations/registration-and-accreditation-netherlands-food-and-consumer-product-safety-authority/
Excerpt: food businesses register each establishment with the NVWA; some handling of
raw animal-origin products requires accreditation. Food-safety/HACCP rules also apply.

GAS, REFRIGERATION AND HEAT-PUMP WORK
URLs:
https://business.gov.nl/regulations/co-certificate-gas-appliances/
https://business.gov.nl/regulations/certificates-working-with-f-gases/
Excerpt: installation, repair or maintenance of gas combustion appliances requires a
CO-certified company and competent technicians. F-gas work requires personal and, for
relevant companies, BRL100 company certification. General construction is not one
licensed profession, but these installation acts are regulated.

VEHICLE INSPECTION
URL: https://business.gov.nl/regulations/performing-periodic-technical-inspections-motor-vehicles-apk/
Excerpt: ordinary car repair is not a protected profession, but conducting APK inspections
requires RDW recognition and an authorised inspector.

FUNERAL TRANSPORT
URL: https://business.gov.nl/regulations/transport-mortal-remains/
Excerpt: cross-border transport requires the death documentation and, outside the relevant
exceptions, a laissez-passer; hearse and coffin requirements apply. Transporting ashes
normally does not require documents, although translated death/cremation certificates are
advisable. Funeral director is not itself a protected profession; regulated transport and
municipal/crematorium processes still require competent Dutch partners.
Related parliamentary record: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-30696-9.html


SOURCE S31 — financial advice and real-estate boundary
Publisher: Netherlands Authority for the Financial Markets (AFM); NVM
Fetch date: 2026-09-03
AFM URL: https://www.afm.nl/nl-nl/sector/adviseurs-bemiddelaars-en-gevolmachtigde-agenten/vergunning-nodig
AFM application URL: https://www.afm.nl/nl-nl/sector/adviseurs-bemiddelaars-en-gevolmachtigde-agenten/vergunning-aanvragen-of-wijzigen/vergunningaanvraag
NVM report URL: https://www.nvm.nl/media/qepftfv1/onderzoeksrapport-samen-febr-21.pdf

Excerpt:
Advice on or mediation in financial products including mortgages and household insurance
requires an AFM licence. Wft diplomas alone are insufficient: conditions also cover fit
management, competent advisers, controlled operations, ownership transparency and,
where relevant, professional-liability insurance. The property-agent title is not currently
statutorily protected (the NVM report discusses whether it should be protected again), but
regulated financial advice and formal valuation activities must be kept out of an unlicensed
buyer/rental concierge.


SOURCE S32 — temporary-employment authorisation
Publisher: Netherlands Enterprise Agency, Business.gov.nl
URL: https://business.gov.nl/amendments/certificate-for-temporary-employment-agencies/
Fetch date: 2026-09-03

Excerpt:
The Wtta authorisation regime is expected to enter into force on 1 January 2027, with
enforcement from 1 January 2028. Agencies must satisfy authorisation conditions including
a €100,000 deposit, VOG, correct pay/tax compliance and approved housing where supplied.

Limit: this page describes a future regime and explicitly says dates were expected at
fetch time; implementation details can still change. The deposit alone consumes the
reader’s stated €100,000 test-capital ceiling.

PROVIDER-CENSUS PROTOCOL
Fetch date for every page below: 2026-09-03

Definition: a “visible provider” is a unique active brand with a public page explicitly
offering Polish, Russian or Ukrainian service, found in the current directory exports
below or in the first results page of a fixed category × language × place web search.
Brands were deduplicated across languages and branches. Counts are lower bounds (≥),
not business-register totals. Social-only providers, inactive pages and companies whose
language could only be inferred from an owner’s name were excluded. Searches covered
Amsterdam, Zaanstad, Haarlem, Utrecht, Rotterdam, The Hague, Westland, Venlo and
Waalwijk, using Polish/Russian/Ukrainian equivalents of each category. Belarusian was
also searched, but no distinct provider set surfaced. The sample cannot see referrals,
Facebook/Telegram-only traders, employees within Dutch practices or search-ranking bias.
